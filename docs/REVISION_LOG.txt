PageBinder revision log
=======================

What changed in each version of PageBinder, newest first.

Each version opens, shows, edits, prints, and searches notebooks made by the
versions before it. Nothing ever needs converting.


Version 1.2.5 - in development (not yet released)
-------------------------------------------------

- Sections and section groups are shown in alphabetical order, in every
  notebook. Pages keep their own order.
- Right-click a section tab, a section group tab, or a page and choose Mark
  inactive. It turns grey and moves to the end of its list, after the
  active ones; everything else about it works as before. Choose Mark active
  to bring it back. Several selected pages can be marked at once.
- Restoring a section or section group from File > Recycled Pages now
  opens it. Before, nothing opened.


Version 1.2.4 - 1 November 2026
-------------------------------

- The font, size, and style buttons at the top now follow the cursor. They
  show the look of the text where the cursor is, wherever it is clicked or
  moved with the keyboard. Before, they could keep showing the look of the
  place before until something was typed.
- A font or size the lists do not offer, such as text pasted from an email
  in 15-point Calibri, is shown as it is, instead of as size 14 or the
  System font.


Version 1.2.3 - 31 October 2026
-------------------------------

- The + for adding a section or section group stays at the right end of
  the section tabs, however many sections a notebook has. The open
  section's tab is scrolled into view.
- The section tabs no longer show a small vertical scroll bar on computers
  that always show scroll bars, such as Windows.


Version 1.2.2 - 27 October 2026 (pre-release)
---------------------------------------------

- Windows: right-clicking a word underlined in red offers suggested
  spellings and Add to dictionary, as on macOS.


Version 1.2.1 - 25 October 2026 (pre-release)
---------------------------------------------

- A page made from a template is named after its first line of text, so it
  needs no naming step, and its name changes as that line is edited. Rename
  the page yourself (right-click > Rename) to give it a fixed name: from then
  on its name stays as you set it.
- Right-click a word underlined in red to see suggested spellings at the top
  of the menu. Choose one to replace the word, or choose Add to dictionary.
- Right-click a notebook in the start screen's Recent list to open its folder
  in Finder (Explorer on Windows), or to remove it from the list.


Version 1.2.0 - 13 October 2026
-------------------------------

The first full release of PageBinder for macOS and Windows. Notebooks are
kept as ordinary folders on your own computer, with freeform pages that
print as they look, file attachments, search, page history, and templates.
